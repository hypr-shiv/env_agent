# INCOIS & IMD Coastal Disaster & Cyclone Emergency Protocols (Palghar Sector)
Document ID: INCOIS-DISASTER-CYCLONE-2024
Author: Indian National Centre for Ocean Information Services (INCOIS) & Palghar District Disaster Management Cell
District: Palghar, Maharashtra

1. Cyclone Warning Four-Stage Alert System:
- Stage 1 (Yellow Watch / Pre-Cyclone Watch): Issued 72 hours before cyclonic disturbance formation over Arabian Sea.
- Stage 2 (Orange Alert / Cyclone Alert): Issued 48 hours prior to expected coastal landfall. Total ban on fishermen venturing into deep sea.
- Stage 3 (Red Warning / Cyclone Warning): Issued 24 hours prior to landfall. Complete beaching of all traditional and mechanized boats. Coastal evacuation initiated for low-lying koliwadas in Dahanu, Satpati, and Arnala.
- Stage 4 (Post-Landfall Outlook): Issued 12 hours prior to landfall until de-escalation.

2. High Swell Surge Advisory:
Swell surges (Kallakkadal) can create breaker waves exceeding 2.5 meters at shallow river mouths during spring high tides without local wind indication. Automatic warning broadcast via VHF Channel 16.

3. Emergency Helplines:

Direct Control Line: 02525-252020 / 252021
