# Pradhan Mantri Matsya Sampada Yojana (PMMSY) - Palghar District Operational Guidelines
Document Type: Official Guideline
Department: Department of Fisheries, Ministry of Fisheries, Animal Husbandry & Dairying, Government of India
District: Palghar, Maharashtra
Publication Date: 2024-04-15

1. Traditional Boat Motorization Scheme:
Under PMMSY Component 1.2, traditional artisanal fishermen operating non-motorized craft (Doni/Hodi) are entitled to financial assistance for procuring Outboard Motors (OBM) or Inboard Motors (IBM) up to 10 Horsepower (HP).
- General Category Beneficiaries: 40% government financial subsidy up to a maximum limit of Rs. 48,000 per engine.
- SC, ST, and Women Beneficiaries: 60% priority financial subsidy up to a maximum limit of Rs. 72,000 per engine.
- Mandatory Prerequisites: Valid Biometric Smart Card issued by the Department of Fisheries, Maharashtra, Form 2 boat registration certificate, Aadhaar-linked bank account, and Fishermen Cooperative Society endorsement.

2. Marine Safety and Communication Transponders:
To ensure open-sea safety, the Central Government covers 60% of the cost of Distress Alert Transmitters (DAT), marine two-way transponders, VHF radios, and life-saving buoyancy jackets for active vessels registered at Satpati, Dahanu, Arnala, and Vasai ports.

3. Subsidized Insulated Ice Boxes:
Up to 60% subsidy is provided for 100-liter and 200-liter insulated ice boxes to reduce post-harvest spoilage on traditional multiday boats.
