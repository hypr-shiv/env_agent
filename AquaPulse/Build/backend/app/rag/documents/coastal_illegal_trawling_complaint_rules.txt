# Maharashtra Marine Fisheries Grievance & Illegal Trawling Enforcement Manual
Document ID: MMFRA-COMPLAINT-ENFORCEMENT-2024
Authority: Department of Fisheries, Government of Maharashtra & Palghar Coastal Police
District: Palghar, Maharashtra

1. Territorial Water Violations:
Under Section 4 of Maharashtra Marine Fishing Regulation Act (MMFRA 1981):
- Mechanized trawlers and purse-seine vessels are strictly prohibited from fishing within 5 nautical miles from the low-water mark along Palghar shoreline (reserved for traditional non-motorized craft).
- High-intensity LED light fishing, pair trawling (bull trawling), and juvenile net harvesting below minimum mesh size (40mm dol net, 65mm gillnet) constitute non-bailable maritime offenses.

2. Grievance Logging & Enforcement Protocol:
- Fishermen observing unauthorized trawlers can lodge complaints through AquaPulse or the 24x7 Coastal Vigilance Cell.
- Required details: GPS location / nautical distance from shoreline, vessel registration markings, photographic or visual description.
- Action Protocol: Fisheries enforcement speedboats based at Satpati and Dahanu dispatch within 4 hours. Violating vessels face license cancellation and seizure of fishing gear.
